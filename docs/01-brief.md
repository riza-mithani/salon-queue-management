# Salon Queue Management System — Project Brief & Scope

## 1. Project Overview

The Salon Queue Management System is a web-based application designed to make salon queue management easier for customers and staff.

Customers can join the queue using their phones, select the services they need and choose a preferred barber. They receive a unique token and can check their queue position and estimated waiting time.

The system provides separate screens for receptionists, barbers and the salon owner to manage daily salon activities.

The salon name has not been finalized and will be decided later.

## 2. Problem Statement

Customers often have to wait at salons without knowing how many people are ahead of them or how long their turn will take. Managing customers manually can also make it difficult for receptionists and barbers to maintain an organized queue.

This system aims to reduce confusion and waiting uncertainty through digital queue management and live status updates.

## 3. Users

- Customers
- Receptionist
- Barbers
- Salon Owner

## 4. Must-have Features

1. **Join Queue:** Customers can enter their name and phone number, select one or more services and choose a preferred barber or Any Barber.
2. **Queue Token:** Generate a unique token for every customer and display their queue position and estimated waiting time.
3. **My Status:** Customers can check their current queue status, receive updates when their turn is near and cancel their queue entry.
4. **Reception Dashboard:** Receptionists can add walk-in customers, view the waiting list, call the next customer and mark customers as No-show or Cancelled.
5. **Barber View:** Barbers can view their assigned customers, start a service, mark it as completed and update their availability.
6. **Waiting Area TV:** Display the barber currently serving each customer and the next five queue tokens.
7. **Owner Settings:** Manage services, service duration, prices, barbers on duty and online queue opening/closing.
8. **Language Support:** Provide English and Gujarati language options throughout the application.

## 5. Available Services

The initial services are:

- Haircut
- Hair Styling
- Hair Spa
- Hair Colouring

The owner can manage service names, prices, durations and availability.

## 6. Staff Access and Login

- Customers can join the queue and check their status without logging in.
- Receptionists access the Reception Dashboard using a staff PIN.
- Barbers access their Barber View using a staff PIN.
- The Salon Owner accesses Owner Settings using a separate owner PIN.
- Staff screens should only be accessible to the appropriate user roles.

## 7. Don't Build

- Online payments
- Future-date appointments
- SMS or WhatsApp notifications
- Loyalty and reward programmes
- Multiple salon branches

## 8. Look and Feel

The website should have a modern, elegant and visually attractive beauty salon appearance, following the supplied design references.

### Background and Colour Scheme

Use the selected five-colour palette:

- Orange-red: #E43D12
- Rose pink: #D6536D
- Soft pink: #FFA2B6
- Golden yellow: #EFB11D
- Warm cream: #EBE9E1

Use warm cream and off-white backgrounds, orange-red for prominent headings and buttons, and rose pink, soft pink and golden yellow for highlights and status indicators.

### Homepage Layout

The homepage should contain:

- A clean navigation bar with a salon name or logo and menu.
- A large hero section featuring a professional beauty-model photograph, attractive heading and Join Queue button.
- An About section with a professional hair-styling photograph and salon introduction.
- A Services section displaying Haircut, Hair Styling, Hair Spa and Hair Colouring.
- A clear call-to-action section for joining the queue.
- Salon information and a footer.

### Photography and Typography

Use two main homepage photographs:

1. A professional beauty-model portrait for the hero banner.
2. A professional hairstyling photograph for the About section.

Use relevant photographs for the four service sections and appropriate barber profile images where required.

Use large, attractive headings, elegant typography, clean body text, generous spacing and simple buttons. Avoid generic dashboard-style designs and excessive coloured cards.

### Responsive Design

The application must work on mobile phones, tablets and desktop screens.

- Reception Dashboard: Tablet-friendly.
- Barber View: Mobile-friendly.
- Waiting Area TV: Large-screen friendly.
- Homepage and Join Queue: Responsive across devices.

All application labels should support English and Gujarati.

## 9. Main Application Screens

The application will contain seven main screens:

1. Home
2. Join Queue
3. My Status
4. Reception Dashboard
5. Barber View
6. Waiting Area TV
7. Owner Settings

Staff PIN access will be provided before entering the relevant staff screens.
